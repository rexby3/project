# Privacy Policy

**Effective date**: 28 May 2026
**Last updated**: 28 May 2026

EgoCore ("we", "us", "our") operates the website https://egocore.ai and the EgoCore mobile applications for iOS and Android (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## 1. Information we collect

### Information you provide
- **Account data**: email address, password (hashed), display name.
- **Content**: messages, journal entries, reflections and other content you create inside the Service.
- **Payment data** (if applicable): processed by our payment provider (Stripe / Apple / Google); we do not store full card numbers.
- **Communications**: emails you send us at hi@egocore.ai.

### Collected automatically
- **Device information**: device model, OS version, app version, language, time zone.
- **Usage data**: pages viewed, features used, crash reports.
- **Push notification token**: a unique identifier from Apple (APNs) or Google (FCM) used to deliver notifications. Linked to your account.
- **IP address**: temporarily, for security and fraud-prevention purposes.
- **Cookies / local storage**: session tokens and feature preferences.

We do **not** collect: precise location, contacts, microphone audio (unless you explicitly start voice input), camera images (unless you upload one), health data, financial account credentials.

## 2. How we use information
- to provide and operate the Service
- to authenticate you and keep your account secure
- to send transactional and product notifications (you can opt out)
- to analyse and improve features
- to detect fraud, abuse and security incidents
- to comply with legal obligations

## 3. AI processing
The Service uses third-party AI providers (e.g. Anthropic, OpenAI) to generate responses. Prompts and context you submit are sent to these providers under their data processing agreements. We do not allow them to train their public models on your content.

## 4. Sharing
We share information only with:
- **Service providers** (hosting, analytics, AI inference, payments) under contractual confidentiality.
- **Authorities** when legally required (subpoena, court order).
- **Successor entities** in case of merger / acquisition (you will be notified).

We do **not** sell personal data.

## 5. Retention
- **Account data**: until you delete your account.
- **Content**: until you delete it or your account.
- **Backups**: removed within 30 days of deletion.
- **Logs**: 90 days.

## 6. Your rights
You can:
- access your data
- correct inaccuracies
- delete your account at any time at https://egocore.ai/account-delete or from the in-app **Settings → Account → Delete Account**
- export your content
- object to processing and request restriction (GDPR / UK GDPR)
- lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise: write to **privacy@egocore.ai**.

## 7. Children
The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. International transfers
Data may be processed in jurisdictions other than yours, including the United States. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

## 9. Security
We use encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt/argon2. No system is 100% secure — please use a strong password and enable biometric lock in the mobile app.

## 10. Changes
We will notify you of material changes via email or in-app banner at least 14 days in advance.
