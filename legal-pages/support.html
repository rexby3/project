Frequently asked questions

How do I reset my password?

Open the app, tap “Forgot password” on the login screen, and follow the email instructions.

How do I enable notifications?

Allow notifications when prompted on first launch, or enable them later in your device Settings → EgoCore → Notifications.

How do I enable Face ID / biometric lock?

In the app, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Lock with Face ID / Fingerprint.

How do I delete my account?

See egocore.ai/account-delete for full instructions.
