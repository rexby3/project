Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 May 2026 · Last updated: 29 May 2026

EgoCore (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the website https://egocore.ai and the EgoCore mobile applications for iOS and Android (collectively, the “Service”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide:

  • Account data: email address, password (stored hashed), display name.
  • Content: messages, reflections and other content you create in the Service.
  • Payment data (if applicable): processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Communications: emails you send to hi@egocore.ai.

Collected automatically:

  • Device information: device model, OS version, app version, language, time zone.
  • Usage data: features used, pages viewed, crash reports.
  • Push notification token: a unique identifier from Apple (APNs) or Google (FCM) used to deliver notifications, linked to your account.
  • IP address: temporarily, for security and fraud prevention.
  • Cookies / local storage: session tokens and preferences.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, microphone audio (unless you explicitly start voice input), or camera images (unless you upload one).

2. How we use information

  • to provide and operate the Service;
  • to authenticate you and keep your account secure;
  • to send transactional and product notifications (you can opt out);
  • to analyse and improve features;
  • to detect fraud, abuse and security incidents;
  • to comply with legal obligations.

3. AI processing

The Service uses third-party AI providers to generate responses. Prompts and context you submit are sent to these providers under their data-processing agreements. We do not permit them to train their public models on your content.

4. Sharing

We share information only with service providers (hosting, analytics, AI inference, payments) under confidentiality obligations, with authorities when legally required, and with a successor entity in case of merger or acquisition (you will be notified). We do not sell personal data.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, export, or delete your data, and object to or restrict processing (GDPR / UK GDPR). You can delete your account at any time at https://egocore.ai/account-delete or from the in-app Settings → Account → Delete Account. To exercise other rights, email privacy@egocore.ai.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. International transfers

Data may be processed in jurisdictions other than yours, including the United States. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt/argon2. No system is 100% secure — please use a strong password and enable biometric lock in the mobile app.

10. Changes

We will notify you of material changes via email or in-app banner at least 14 days in advance.
