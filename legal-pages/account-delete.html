Delete your EgoCore account

You can permanently delete your EgoCore account and associated data at any time. This page explains how, and what gets removed.

Option 1 — In the app

  1. Open the EgoCore app.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address linked to your account:

Request account deletion

We will verify your identity and process the request within 30 days.

What is deleted

DataWhen
Account profile (email, name)Immediately
Your content (messages, reflections)Immediately
Push notification tokensImmediately
Backups containing your dataWithin 30 days

What may be retained

We may keep limited records required by law (e.g. transaction/tax records) for the period mandated by applicable regulations. These are not used for any other purpose.
